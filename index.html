About Me

I am Dr. Siva Raja P. M., a Professor in Computer Science & Engineering at Amrita College of Engineering & Technology. I hold a Ph.D. in CSE from Satyabhama Institute of Science and Technology (2018), M.Tech from Manonmaniam Sundaranar University (2009), and B.Tech in IT from Sun College (2005).

With 18+ years of academic and research experience, I specialize in Medical Image Processing, Deep Learning, and Blockchain Security. I’ve received multiple awards for excellence in research and faculty performance.

I am a life member of ISTE, and a professional member of IEEE and ACM.
